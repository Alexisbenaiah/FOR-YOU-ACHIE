A little something for you

For Someone Special (Achie)

Hindi ko alam kung paano ko ito sasabihin... kaya naisip kong gumawa na lang ng maliit na lugar para sa mga salitang hindi ko masabi nang diretso.

We haven't known each other for that long. Sa totoo lang, lately lang tayo nagkakilala through an app.

Pero somehow, may kakaiba sa'yo. Hindi ko ma-explain kung paano o bakit, pero sa maikling panahon na nakilala kita, parang may something sa'yo na hindi ko basta ma-ignore.

Hindi naman kita gustong madaliin. Hindi rin ako gumagawa ng malaking expectation. Gusto ko lang maging honest sa isang bagay...

You became special to me faster than I expected. ♡

Siguro dahil sa way ng pakikipag-usap mo, sa little things na ginagawa mo, sa comfort na nararamdaman ko kapag kausap kita, at sa feeling na parang ang gaan ng mundo kahit saglit lang.

Nakakatuwa lang isipin na isang taong hindi ko naman inaasahang makikilala ko, suddenly became someone na hinihintay kong makausap.

Hindi ko alam kung saan tayo dadalhin ng mga susunod na araw. Maybe we'll become closer, maybe we'll simply remain two people who crossed paths.

But whatever happens... I'm genuinely thankful that I met you.

Kung sakaling hindi ko man masabi sa'yo nang harapan, gusto kong malaman mo ito:

I'm really happy that I met you.

At kahit maikli pa lang ang panahon, somehow you already left a beautiful little space in my thoughts.

Hindi ko kailangan ng mabilis na sagot. Hindi ko rin kailangan malaman agad kung ano ang magiging ending nito.

For now, I just want to enjoy knowing you, talking to you, laughing with you, and discovering the person behind the screen.

And maybe someday, kapag mas matagal na tayong magkakilala, babalikan natin itong maliit na website na 'to at matatawa tayo kung gaano ka-simple pero ka-special ang simula.

Thank you for coming into my life. ♡
— From someone who didn't expect to meet you, but is really glad he did. 💗 © Alexis
